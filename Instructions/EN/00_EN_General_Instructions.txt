Welcome to the Trail Making Test in Virtual Reality.
This test consists of two main parts: Task A, and Task B.
For each part, you will first complete a short tutorial.
Then, you will do a practice trial.
Finally, you will do the actual test.
The tutorials and practice trials are just to help you get comfortable. They are not the main measurement.
To select a cube target, keep your aim on the target for one second.
If you select the correct target, it will turn blue, and you will hear a confirmation sound.
If you select the wrong target, it will briefly turn red with an error sound. Then it will return to normal.
Lines may appear between correct targets to show your progress.
Please work as quickly, and accurately, as you can.
