This is the tutorial for Task A. Pointer mode. Aim at the cube labeled 1 using your controller, or your index finger. Hold on the center of the cube for one second to select it. Then select 2, then 3, and so on, until you have selected all eight tutorial targets. If you choose the wrong target, it will turn red. Simply select the correct next target. Are you ready? The tutorial starts in 3… 2… 1… Go.
