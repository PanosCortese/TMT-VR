This is the tutorial for Task B. Pointer mode. Aim at 1 using your controller, or your index finger. Hold for one second to select. Next, aim at A, and hold for one second to select. Continue by alternating numbers and letters: 1… A… 2… B… 3… C… 4… D… and so on, until you have selected all eight tutorial targets. If you choose the wrong target, it will turn red. Simply select the correct next target. Are you ready? The tutorial starts in 3… 2… 1… Go.
