## Spoken instructions

The spoken instructions were synthesised from the instruction script with Google Cloud Text-to-Speech. No human voice was recorded. The instruction script is included as text files (`Instructions/`), so the instructions can be translated and synthesised in another language (see README).

## Sound effects

The three feedback sounds come from Freesound. "Wrong Buzzer" (KevinVG207) and "reception bell1.mp3" (kleinhirn2000) are CC0 1.0 and need no attribution. They are credited here as a courtesy. "Confirmation Downward" by original_sound is licensed under CC BY 3.0 and is credited as that licence requires. In this project it is `Confirm.wav`, 2.02 s long; the original is 2.00 s.
